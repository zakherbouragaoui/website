# Environmental Governance in Tunisia

Environmental governance plays a crucial role in protecting Tunisia's natural resources and biodiversity.

## Current State

Tunisia has comprehensive environmental laws but enforcement remains challenging. Opportunities exist through democratic transition, international support, and technology.

## The Path Forward

Progress requires strengthened enforcement, coordination, public participation, and transparency.
